Hey! You can call me Gus.

I’m a 25-year-old designer based in Recife, Brazil.

Over the past few years I’ve been building experience at the intersection of branding, creative direction, and product design. My goal is to turn business strategy into cohesive and actionable narratives, from products to sales.

In my role, I always look forward to collaborating with multidisciplinary teams and combining different tools, methodologies, and references to maximize the coherence, results, and impact of my work. As a challenge-driven professional, I’m always looking forward to create something new and learn from it along the way.

I design because I’m a storyteller, an organizer and a semiotics nerd. I like to think that I speak with shapes, colors, space, light and movement to say what’s beyond words.
